About Us

Kips Restaurant and Ice Cream Parlor started small in Blantyre, Malawi, selling just three chickens a day with only eight tables. Over the years, we've grown to become a beloved spot for fast food, pizzas, and ice cream across Malawi. We focus on delicious meals like crispy chicken, nsima with roasted chicken, and a variety of pizzas and desserts. Our mission is to provide great food every single time with good vibes.
